Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before hiring an AI automation consultant.

What is n8n and how can it automate my business? +

n8n is an open-source workflow automation tool that connects your business apps — CRMs, email, databases, APIs — without manual work. I build custom n8n workflows that eliminate repetitive tasks like lead follow-up, data entry, and reporting, saving your team 10–30 hours per week.

What is the difference between n8n and Make.com? +

n8n is self-hostable and more flexible for complex workflows and custom code, while Make.com is a cloud-based platform with a lower barrier to entry. I work with both and recommend the right tool based on your budget, technical environment, and workflow complexity.

How long does it take to build a custom automation workflow? +

Most workflows are delivered in 3–10 days depending on complexity. Simple integrations (e.g. form → CRM → email) take 1–3 days. Complex multi-step pipelines with AI, web scraping, or voice agents take 1–2 weeks. I offer a free demo before any commitment.

Can you automate lead generation for my sales team? +

Yes. I build end-to-end lead generation automation pipelines — scraping prospects from Google Maps, LinkedIn, or custom sources, cleaning and enriching the data, and routing qualified leads directly into your CRM or outreach tool.

Do I need technical knowledge to use your services? +

No. I handle all the technical setup, testing, and documentation. You describe the process you want automated and I build it. After delivery I provide clear documentation so your team can monitor and manage it without any coding knowledge.

What tools and platforms do you integrate with? +

I integrate with 200+ platforms including HubSpot, Airtable, Notion, Slack, Google Sheets, OpenAI, Twilio, and any platform with an API. I also build custom Python scrapers for sites without official APIs.
